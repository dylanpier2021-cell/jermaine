About

One name behind every project.

Jermaine built this business around a clear observation: home improvement projects regularly span multiple trades, and the handoffs between separate contractors are where quality and accountability get lost.

When you hire this firm, painting, flooring, carpet, remodeling, and make-ready work are all overseen by one person. Jermaine handles every project from the initial walk-through to the final sign-off.

Questions

Common questions.

Do you really handle all these services in-house?

Yes. Painting, carpet cleaning, flooring, remodeling, and make-ready units are all performed by our own team. We do not subcontract.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, fully licensed and insured. Certificates of insurance are available on request before any project begins.

How quickly can you start a project?

Most projects can begin within two weeks of the estimate. Availability varies by season and current workload. Contact us to check current availability.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. All estimates are free, written, and include a detailed scope of work. No obligation to proceed.
