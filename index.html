About

One name behind
every project.

Jermaine Williams founded Illini Cleaning and Painting in 2006 on a straightforward principle: most home improvement projects involve multiple trades, and coordinating separate contractors creates scheduling friction, handoff gaps, and no single person accountable for the finished result.

With one team covering painting, flooring, carpet, remodeling, and make-ready, the coordination happens internally. Jermaine oversees every project personally, from the initial walk-through to the final sign-off, serving Champaign, Urbana, and surrounding Central Illinois.

Questions

Common questions.

Do you really handle all these services in-house?

Yes. Painting, carpet cleaning, flooring, remodeling, and make-ready units are all performed by our own team. We do not subcontract.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, fully licensed and insured. Certificates of insurance are available on request before any project begins.

How quickly can you start a project?

Most projects can begin within two weeks of the estimate. Availability varies by season and current workload. Contact us to check current availability.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. All estimates are free, written, and include a detailed scope of work. No obligation to proceed. We schedule consultations with at least one day's notice and offer phone, video, or in-person options.

What service area do you cover?

We serve Champaign, Urbana, Decatur, Danville, Tolono, Savoy, and surrounding Central Illinois. We travel up to 100 miles depending on the job. Contact us to confirm availability in your area.
