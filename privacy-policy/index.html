Last updated: 2026/06/06

This Privacy Policy describes how Illini Cleaning and Painting, a sole proprietorship owned by Jermaine Williams ("Company," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our website or contact us for services.

Definitions

  • Company means Illini Cleaning and Painting, a sole proprietorship owned by Jermaine Williams, located at 2701 Willowpark Dr, Champaign, IL .
  • Service means the Company website at illinicleaningandpainting.com and any advertisements or communications operated by the Company.
  • Personal Data means any information that relates to an identified or identifiable individual.
  • You means the individual accessing the Service or, where applicable, a company or entity on behalf of which that individual is accessing the Service.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of personal data when you contact us, submit a form, or engage with our Service:

  • First and last name

  • Comments, project descriptions, or other information you voluntarily provide

We do not collect payment card data directly. We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising purposes beyond basic functionality.

How We Use Your Information

We use the personal data we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve our services

  • To comply with legal obligations and protect the security of our operations

Mobile Communication Policy

When you initiate contact through our website chat widget or verbally agree to receive text messages, you consent to receive SMS communications from Illini Cleaning and Painting. These messages may include appointment reminders, booking confirmations, payment reminders, and service notifications.

Mobile opt-in data and consent are never shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Message frequency varies depending on your interactions with us.
  • To opt out of SMS messages at any time, reply STOP to any message you receive from us.
  • For help, reply HELP or contact us directly.

We take reasonable precautions to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.
