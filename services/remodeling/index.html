Remodeling without the contractor coordination headache.

One team, one estimate, one call. Concept through completion in Central Illinois since 2006.

Kitchens, bathrooms, and full-room transformations.

Concept through completion with a single point of contact. Because painting, flooring, and tile are all handled in-house, there is no waiting for one crew to finish before another can start, and no finger-pointing when finish work does not align.

Most remodeling delays come from coordinating multiple contractors who each have their own schedules and priorities. We handle the finish trades in-house, demo through final coat, so your timeline is ours to manage. You get one estimate, one schedule, and one person to call.

  • Drywall, painting, flooring, and tile combined
  • One point of contact for decisions and updates

What to expect

1. Consultation and scope

We walk through the space with you, discuss your goals and budget, and identify every trade involved. You receive a written scope covering all work before anything begins.

2. Sequenced execution

Demolition, structural prep, flooring, tile, and paint are done in the correct order by our own crew. No handoffs, no scheduling gaps between trades.

3. Final walkthrough

We walk the finished space with you before the job is closed. Any corrections are handled before we leave, not weeks later.
